Dr. A.P.J Abdul Kalam

About

Dr. A.P.J Abdul Kalam was the 11th President of India and a famous scientist. He is known as the "Missile Man of India" for his work in developing missile technology.

Achievements

  • Developed India's missile programs
  • Played key role in Pokhran-II nuclear tests
  • Received Bharat Ratna in 1997
  • Inspired millions of students

Message

"Dream, Dream, Dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."
